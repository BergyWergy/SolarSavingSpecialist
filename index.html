Storage that pays

Keep today's sunshine for tonight

Most homes use the least power in the middle of the day, just when panels generate the most. A well-sized battery holds on to that energy so you can use it in the evening instead of buying it back from the grid.

  • Use more of your own power from the same panels
  • Charge on cheaper overnight tariffs where it helps
  • Backup-capable options for power cuts
  • Live monitoring of generation, storage and savings
